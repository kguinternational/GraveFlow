The Process

Three steps.
One promise.

From order to verified proof of care — automated, transparent, and trustworthy.

1
🛒

Order

A Family Member searches the decentralized BillionGraves registry node, selects a personalized honor package, and locks the escrow via a zero-knowledge smart contract. Values remain state-locked on-chain — zero counterparty risk.

2
📷

Verify

A nearby Caregiver accepts the dispatch, navigates via offline-first cryptographic telemetry, and submits multi-angle visual proofs. The local-first LLaVA vision engine verifies headstone cleaning and floral placement instantly.

3
💸

Payout

Upon instant local AI verification, the smart contract automatically executes and unlocks funds. The Caregiver is compensated in gasless stable tokens, the Family Member receives their immutable proof-of-care NFT, and the public sovereign ledger updates instantly.
