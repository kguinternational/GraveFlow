# Project Resilience: The Love Effect OS™ Onboarding Stack

To create a high-fidelity Psychological Digital Twin for child isolation remediation and trauma resilience, the following assessments form the complete "Getting to Know You" onboarding sequence.

## Core Identity & Personality
1. **See My Personality (Big 5)**: Baseline traits (Openness, Conscientiousness, Extroversion, Agreeableness, Neuroticism).
2. **16 Personalities (MBTI)**: Cognitive processing and decision-making framework.
3. **The Enneagram (Riso-Hudson)**: Core fears, desires, and defense mechanisms.

## Relational & Emotional Connectivity
4. **The 5 Love Languages**: How the individual receives and processes care/affection.
5. **Personal Development School Attachment Style**: How they form bonds and react to relational stress (crucial for isolation).
6. **Emotional Intelligence (EQ)**: *[Added]* Measures emotional regulation, self-awareness, and empathy.

## Growth, Action & Value Creation
7. **VAK Learning Style**: How they ingest and retain information (Visual, Auditory, Kinesthetic).
8. **The Millionaire Master Plan (Wealth Dynamics)**: Their natural path of least resistance for value creation and flow.
9. **The VIA Survey of Character Strengths**: Innate moral virtues and positive psychological baseline.

## Resilience & Trauma Context *(Added for Project Resilience)*
10. **Brief Resilience Scale (BRS)**: *[Added]* Directly measures the ability to bounce back from stress and adversity. Establishes the core metric that Project Resilience aims to improve over time.
11. **Locus of Control (Rotter)**: *[Added]* Determines if the individual feels they control their own destiny (Internal) or if they feel helpless against external forces (External). Critical for shifting isolated youth from victimhood to empowerment.
12. **Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACE) / Trauma Baseline**: *[Added]* A gentle, trauma-informed baseline to understand the environmental stressors and historical trauma load the individual is carrying.
